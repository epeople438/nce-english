🔑 本课语法重点：复数 be 动词

I am → We are（我们是）
He / She is → They are（他们是）
Are you...? → Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
Are they...? → Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Are you Danish? → No, we are not. We are Norwegian.
