Customs Officer 海关官员
Are you Swedish?
你们是瑞典人吗？
Girls 姑娘们
No, we are not. We are Danish.
不，我们不是瑞典人。我们是丹麦人。
Customs Officer 海关官员
Are your friends Danish, too?
你们的朋友也是丹麦人吗？
Girls 姑娘们
No, they aren't. They are Norwegian.
不，他们不是。他们是挪威人。
Customs Officer 海关官员
Your passports, please.
请出示你们的护照。
Girls 姑娘们
Here they are.
给您。
Customs Officer 海关官员
Are these your cases?
这些是你们的箱子吗？
Girls 姑娘们
No, they aren't. Our cases are brown. Here they are.
不，不是。我们的箱子是棕色的。给您看。
Customs Officer 海关官员
Are you tourists?
你们是游客吗？
Girls 姑娘们
Yes, we are.
是的，我们是。
Customs Officer 海关官员
Are your friends tourists, too?
你们的朋友也是游客吗？
Girls 姑娘们
Yes, they are.
是的，他们也是。
Customs Officer 海关官员
That's fine.
好的。
Girls 姑娘们
Thank you very much.
非常感谢。
