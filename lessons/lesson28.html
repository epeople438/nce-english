📖 本课要点复习

✅ Where is it? → It is near / on…（单数）
✅ Where are they? → They are on / near…（复数）
✅ magazines → on the television
✅ newspapers → on the table
✅ armchairs → near the table
✅ books → on the stereo
✅ pictures → on the wall
